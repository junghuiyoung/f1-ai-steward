    AI Steward's answer

    AI Steward answers from the rules and from earlier stewards' decisions only. It has no video, no telemetry and no hearing, so it gives the usual outcome and the stewards may decide otherwise.

    Rule and decision documents belong to the FIA. This repository doesn't include them; a script downloads each one from fia.com to your machine, and the app runs locally.

    How it was evaluated, and how often it agrees with the stewards: see the README.
